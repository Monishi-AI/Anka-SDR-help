💢Pain Point #1 — Denial Management: The Execution Math

Denials — the core execution gap. Reports and recommendations don't recover a dollar; execution does.

The execution math — memorize this
At 1–4 hours per manual appeal, one FTE clears roughly 500–600 denials a month — and reaches only ~8 payers a day. A mid-market organization generates denials far faster than that, and 65% of denied claims are never reworked (~$700 of effort each). Anka works 5,000+ denials a month and the entire payer queue. The rest ages out — permanent revenue loss. This is a math problem, not a people problem.

Root causes

No dedicated denial staff (1–3 people handling everything) · No systematic root-cause tracking · Appeal deadlines (90 days) pass while claims sit in queue · ~90% of denials are preventable but no prevention infrastructure exists.

Anka’s response

Reads EOB → identifies denial reason → drafts payer-specific appeal → submits electronically via payer portal → tracks response timeline → follows up if no response. Zero hand-offs. All logged.

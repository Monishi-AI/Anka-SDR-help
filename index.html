P

Re-emphasizing outcomes over standard fees. Fees adjust if performance targets are missed — paid for results, not activity.

O vs P — they are not the same pillar
O is the commercial model (how ANKA charges — outcome-based contracts, $0 upfront). P is the performance guarantee (what happens against targets — SLAs, fee adjustments, proof of results). O opens the pricing conversation; P closes the trust gap.
